ABOUT

BookTracker is a platform designed to accompany and motivate readers along their journey. It allows users to record the books they’ve read, visually track their progress, and discover personalized statistics that show how their reading habits have evolved over time.

Inspired by the idea of a “Reading Wrapped,” BookTracker transforms the reading experience into something more dynamic and meaningful. Users can explore data such as their favorite genres, most-read authors, number of books per month, and more — all within a clear, modern interface designed to provide a pleasant experience.

The goal of BookTracker is to offer a simple and functional tool for readers who want to monitor their reading habits, visualize their daily progress, and keep all their books organized in one place.

Built with Django, Python, and SQLite, BookTracker provides a friendly and accessible interface for all types of users.
